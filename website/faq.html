FAQ

How do I sign in?

Use email and password, or Google. Phone login is not part of this launch.

Where are my tasks stored?

In your Study Flow account, protected by row-level security so you only see your own data.

Does Focus count if I reset?

No. Only naturally completed sessions are saved.

How do subscriptions work?

Basic is free forever with 20 AI generations per day and ads. Pro is ₹299/month or ₹2,500/year with 80 AI generations per day and no ads. Android Pro subscriptions use Google Play Billing. Restore purchases from the Subscription screen.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account, or see the account deletion page.

FAQ

How do I sign in?

Use email and password, or Google. Phone login is not part of this launch.

Where are my tasks stored?

In your Study Flow account, protected by row-level security so you only see your own data.

Does Focus count if I reset?

No. Only naturally completed sessions are saved.

How do subscriptions work?

Basic is free forever. Pro is ₹299/month or ₹2,500/year through Google Play. Restore purchases from the Subscription screen.

Can I use Leo on Basic?

Yes. Leo is included on Basic and Pro. Basic has 20 AI generations per day. Pro has 80. Ads show on Basic only.

Can I add a profile picture?

Yes. Open Settings, then add or change your photo. It is saved to your account.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account, or see the account deletion page.
